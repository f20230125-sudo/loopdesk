How to read these numbers

Prototype definitions
Open opportunities
Current stage is New, Contacted, Qualified or Proposal. Archived organisations and contacts retain their linked opportunities and history.
Overdue follow-ups
Incomplete tasks with a due date before today in Asia/Dubai (UTC+4). Tasks on closed opportunities remain due until completed.
Won recorded estimates
The sum of known estimated AED values for opportunities currently marked Won. This is not recognised revenue. Unknown values are shown separately; zero stays zero.
Recorded outcomes
Counts of opportunities currently marked Won or Lost. This is a current snapshot, not a conversion rate or historical revenue report.
Inactive open opportunities
No recorded activity, edit or stage change for at least {{ health.stale_days }} elapsed days, using the latest timestamp or creation; unknown dates are excluded and counted separately.
Average days in current stage
Mean elapsed days since the latest stage event, falling back to creation only when no event exists, for open opportunities in each stage; an empty stage or unavailable date is unknown.
Recent stage changes
Events with a previous stage, grouped by destination, from midnight in Asia/Dubai {{ health.window_days - 1 }} days ago through now; initial creation events are excluded and unavailable dates are unknown.
Recent completed and due follow-ups
Distinct task IDs with completion audit entries in the same {{ health.window_days }} Dubai calendar days (including later reopenings), versus tasks whose current due date is in those dates, regardless of status; legacy completion entries without task IDs have unknown attribution.
