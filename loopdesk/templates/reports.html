How to read these numbers

Prototype definitions
Open opportunities
Current stage is New, Contacted, Qualified or Proposal. Archived organisations and contacts retain their linked opportunities and history.
Overdue follow-ups
Incomplete tasks with a due date before today in Asia/Dubai (UTC+4). Tasks on closed opportunities remain due until completed.
Won recorded estimates
The sum of known estimated AED values for opportunities currently marked Won. This is not recognised revenue. Unknown values are shown separately; zero stays zero.
Recorded outcomes
Counts of opportunities currently marked Won or Lost. This is a current snapshot, not a conversion rate or historical revenue report.
